## Project Purpose
Valoura — shipped as the **Contextual Valuation Engine (CVE)** — is a
context-specific stock valuation algorithm for technology stocks. Every ticker is
classified along three independent axes, and valuation logic is applied
conditionally based on the resulting cell.

Two halves:
1. **Offline pipeline** (`data_pipeline1.py`) — ingests fundamentals, computes
   metrics, classifies, values, and writes to local SQLite. Re-run as thresholds
   are calibrated.
2. **Web app** (`Valuora_Previous_UI/app.py`) — Streamlit front end with Supabase
   accounts, per-user watchlists, and stage-change email alerts. Reads the SQLite
   DB; never writes to it.

NOT financial advice. NOT a live trading system.

## The Three-Classifier Architecture

Classifiers are deliberately independent — combining them loses signal.

---

### Classifier 1 — Business Model (BM)
Five mutually exclusive categories evaluated in strict priority order. First match wins.

| Priority | Category | Key Signal |
|----------|----------|------------|
| 1 | `saas` | subscription ≥70%, gross margin ≥65%, deferred revenue ratio ≥5%, no owned infra, hardware <10% |
| 2 | `hyperscale` | owns infra = yes, usage-based ≥25%, gross margin ≥55% |
| 3 | `semi_hardware` | hardware ≥35% OR (gross margin <55% AND ad revenue <40%) |
| 4 | `consumer_internet` | advertising ≥40% (POSITIVE rule, not catch-all) |
| 5 | `deep_tech` | no dominant revenue stream ≥40%, or pre-revenue |

**Classification method** — hybrid rule-based + LLM:
- Rule-based balance-sheet validators run first (one per category)
- LLM (Gemini) called ONLY to break ties: 2+ validators pass, OR 0 pass, OR confidence < 0.80
- `MANUAL_BM_OVERRIDES` short-circuits both validators and LLM (used when AV data
  structurally can't pass a correct validator — e.g. MSFT PPE not exposed by AV,
  or when an LLM tiebreaker lands on a category that contradicts the company's
  actual business model). 19 entries as of 2026-08:
  ```python
  MANUAL_BM_OVERRIDES = {
      # Infra owners AV can't validate (PPE not exposed)
      "MSFT": "hyperscale", "AMZN": "hyperscale", "GOOGL": "hyperscale",
      "ORCL": "hyperscale", "IBM": "hyperscale",
      # "Neocloud" AI hyperscalers — own GPU data centres, usage-based revenue.
      # Validators read their SaaS-like margins and miss the CapEx intensity.
      "CRWV": "hyperscale", "NBIS": "hyperscale",
      # Subscription software the validators/LLM mislabelled
      "SNOW": "saas", "MDB": "saas", "DOCN": "saas", "U": "saas",
      # Semiconductor / hardware names that LLM tiebreaks sent to deep_tech
      "INTC": "semi_hardware", "ADI": "semi_hardware", "NTAP": "semi_hardware",
      # Consumer platforms mislabelled deep_tech (AR/gaming/gig R&D signals)
      "DASH": "consumer_internet", "SNAP": "consumer_internet",
      "TTWO": "consumer_internet",
      # Frontier R&D with zero-inventory/zero-PPE profiles that look SaaS-like
      "QBTS": "deep_tech", "BKSY": "deep_tech",
  }
  ```
  **Override policy:** always review a `LOW`-confidence result, and override when
  the assigned category contradicts the company's primary business model. A LOW
  confidence score alone is not grounds for an override — the category has to be
  wrong. (See *Known Open Items* for the deferred hyperscale category split.)
- `bm_method` values logged per classification: `validator`, `llm_tiebreaker`,
  `override`, `llm_post_saas_demotion_zero_pass`

**SaaS post-classification gate** — a ticker classified `saas` by validators or LLM
must additionally clear ONE of:
1. `deferred_revenue_ratio >= 0.05` (5% of LTM revenue — billed-in-advance proxy)
2. ARR value present in `manual_metrics.json`
3. Listed in `MANUAL_BM_OVERRIDES` as "saas"

Failure demotes to next best non-saas category (LLM re-called on remaining
candidates). This prevents ad-driven or hardware companies riding a loose SaaS label.
Threshold 5% separates true SaaS (CRM/NET/ADBE/SNOW at 33-72%) from zero-deferred
companies like RDDT (1.1%) that got saas from the LLM.

### Classifier 2 — Financial Health (FH) Stage (1–4)
Each metric independently maps to a stage 1–4, then weighted-averaged. Result
rounded to nearest integer → `fh_stage`.

**Stage definitions (anchor = FCF margin adjusted for SBC):**
| Stage | Name | FCF margin adj |
|-------|------|----------------|
| S1 | Cash burning | < -15% |
| S2 | Growth investing | -15% to +8% |
| S3 | FCF positive | +8% to +22% |
| S4 | Cash compounding | > +22% |

**HARD CAP:** if FCF margin adj = Stage 1, overall stage cannot exceed Stage 2
regardless of other metrics. Prevents high-GM companies burning heavily on S&M
from being misclassified as healthy.

**Weights (must sum to 100% per column):**
| Metric | S1 | S2 | S3 | S4 |
|--------|----|----|----|-----|
| FCF margin adj | 30 | 30 | 30 | 35 |
| Cash runway | 45 | 15 | 0 | 0 |
| Gross margin | 15 | 15 | 15 | 15 |
| Operating leverage | 0 | 25 | 20 | 10 |
| SBC % revenue | 5 | 10 | 10 | 5 |
| Debt/equity | 5 | 5 | 10 | 15 |
| ROIC | 0 | 0 | 15 | 20 |

**Data quality flags:**
- `net_income_quality_flag=1` fires when `NI` diverges materially from
  `OpInc*(1-tax_rate)` — signals non-cash items (goodwill, IP gains) distorting NI.
  Affects MRVL (large non-cash gain → cycle P/E inflated to 3000+x).
- `excluded_currency`: tickers where `yfinance.financialCurrency` is not USD are
  fully skipped (TSM=TWD, ASML=EUR). Currency authority is yfinance, NOT AV.
- Negative book equity: `debt_equity` returns None when equity ≤ 0 (buyback-heavy
  mature companies). Score neutral, do not penalise.
- `MIN_PRIOR_BASE_REVENUE = $10M`: growth rates nulled out when prior-LTM revenue
  below threshold (JOBY went $111k→$77M = 69,874% — technically correct, useless).

### v2 change log (from v1)
- **hyperscale-2:** CapEx EV/EBIT 40–80x removed → EV/NTM Rev primary. The
  CapEx-adjusted lens was structurally wrong at S2 where depreciation dominates.
- **hyperscale-2 floor 6x → 3x (v2.1):** at a 6x floor, mature hyperscalers
  scored *Undervalued* on scale rather than cheapness (AMZN 3.4x, IBM 3.6x).
  Both now read *Fair*.
- **consumer_internet-4:** P/E added as PRIMARY ahead of EV/EBITDA.
- **deep_tech-1:** `P/S` renamed and re-keyed to `EV/NTM Rev` (same 20–60x band).
  **No `P/S` string remains in that cell** — verdict and source-label lookups key
  on the metric-name string, so the rename had to be total.
- **semi_hardware-3:** Rule of 40 dropped from fair ranges (Cycle P/E + PEG only).
- **S4 FCF-yield retune:** hyperscale-4 2.0–4.0, saas-4 2.0–3.5,
  semi_hardware-4 2.5–4.5, deep_tech-4 2.0–4.0 (+PEG).

### deep_tech-1 pre-revenue fallback
EV/NTM Rev divides by ~zero for pre-revenue names. When `revenue` is null or ≤ 0
at deep_tech-1, the cell switches to:
- `primary_method = "EV/Cash and Cash Runway"`
- `ev_to_cash = enterprise_value / cash_and_equivalents`
- `metric_label = "EV/Cash"` (so the UI labels it correctly)
- `cash_runway_months` surfaced as the supporting signal

This is the zero-division guard for pre-revenue names. `EV/Cash` routes through
the **ratio** branch of `get_verdict()` (lower = cheaper), as do `Cycle_PE` and
all EV/x multiples; `FCF_yield` routes through the **yield** branch
(higher = better).

### FLAG_peg_value_trap
Computed wherever PEG is a cell metric. Fires when **PEG < 0.8 AND LTM YoY
revenue growth is below the prior quarter's** (decelerating). Writes
`FLAG_peg_value_trap` into `valuation_json` with the note *"Low PEG with
decelerating growth — may indicate a value trap rather than undervaluation."*
and surfaces it in the app's Risk Flags panel. The PEG value itself is never
suppressed — both are shown. Mirrors the RPO two-quarter-persistence idea:
direction matters, not the level alone.

### Valuation matrix — general rules
- PEG applies at S3 and S4 only; never S1/S2 (undefined on near-zero earnings)
- R40 (Rule of 40 = revenue growth + FCF margin) is context, never a primary multiple
- Zero-division guard mandatory: `value = num/denom if denom and denom != 0 else None`
  Return None and display "N/A — pre-revenue". Never crash.

## RPO Qualifier System

RPO (Remaining Performance Obligations) is an ASC 606 accounting disclosure —
formal measure of contracted future revenue not yet recognised.

**Extraction:** `metrics_extractor.py` — keyword-window approach for 10-Qs/10-Ks.
Searches for RPO section using `_RPO_KEYWORDS`, extracts ±3,000 chars around each
match, up to 40,000 chars total. Avoids blind head-truncation that missed Notes
sections in large (8-31MB) SEC HTML filings.

Keywords searched:
```
"remaining performance obligation"
"remaining performance obligations"
"transaction price allocated to remaining"
"unsatisfied performance obligation"
```

**Metrics computed** (stored in `computed_metrics`):
- `rpo` — current-period RPO (from `manual_metrics.json`)
- `rpo_prior_year` — same-quarter prior year RPO
- `rpo_growth_yoy` — `(rpo / rpo_prior_year - 1)` × 100 (%)
- `rpo_revenue_spread` — `rpo_growth_yoy - revenue_growth_yoy` (percentage points)

**Qualifier states:**
| State | Condition | Meaning |
|-------|-----------|---------|
| `forward_demand_ahead` | spread > +5pp | RPO outpacing revenue — contracted backlog building |
| `neutral` | -5pp ≤ spread ≤ +5pp, OR single-quarter negative | No decisive signal |
| `forward_demand_decelerating` | spread < 0pp for 2+ consecutive quarters | Contracted pipeline contracting |

**Qualifier cells:**
- **Primary role:** hyperscale-S2, hyperscale-S3 (RPO is critical forward demand signal)
- **Secondary role:** saas-S2, saas-S3 (supplementary context, not primary multiple)
- No role: semi_hardware, consumer_internet, deep_tech (RPO not standard in these models)

**Known state (as of 2026-05-21):**
- `rpo_prior_year` still NULL for most tickers (only 1 prior-year extraction cycle
  completed). Will self-resolve next quarter when current values become prior_year.
- PANW: -14.2pp spread, single quarter → `neutral` (not yet `forward_demand_decelerating`)
- SNOW: +13.1pp spread → `forward_demand_ahead` ✓ (primary multiple may understate)
- ADBE: +1.9pp → `neutral` (flat, no directional signal)

## Data Architecture

> ⚠ **Before writing any code against AV, read the *AV Data Defects* section
> below.** AV silently returns wrong-but-plausible data in nine documented ways
> (frozen delisted prices, `"None"` strings, bogus delisting dates, discarded
> fundamentals, reused tickers). Each one has cost real debugging time.

### Primary financial API — Alpha Vantage (AV)
Quarterly fundamentals: INCOME_STATEMENT, CASH_FLOW, BALANCE_SHEET, **EARNINGS**.

**EARNINGS is the only endpoint carrying a filing date** (`reportedDate`). The
three statement endpoints do not, which is why `backfill_fundamentals.py`
fetches EARNINGS first and stamps `fundamentals.reported_date` by fiscal period.
That column is what makes point-in-time backtesting honest — without it the
backtester falls back to a flat 45-day-after-fiscal-end guess.

**Field-name traps (both cost real money in wrong valuations):**
- D&A is `depreciationDepletionAndAmortization`, **not** `depreciationAmortization`.
  Reading the wrong name silently returns 0, collapsing EBITDA into operating
  income and understating every EV/EBITDA cell.
- Historical shares outstanding are in the BALANCE_SHEET as
  `commonStockSharesOutstanding`. Use these for point-in-time market cap / EV —
  `backtester.py` previously used *today's* share count for all history.
- The live pipeline caps fundamentals at 20 quarters (`YEARS_OF_DATA * 4`).
  `backfill_fundamentals.py` deliberately does **not**, and pulls the full
  history AV holds (~81 quarters for mature names).

**Tier: PRO ($50/month) — 75 req/min, NO daily cap.** Active since 2026-08.

```python
AV_CALLS_PER_MINUTE = int(os.getenv("AV_CALLS_PER_MINUTE", "75"))  # Pro is the default
AV_SLEEP = 60.0 / AV_CALLS_PER_MINUTE                              # 0.8s at 75/min
```

No env export is required — the engine defaults to Pro. `~/.zshrc` also sets
`AV_CALLS_PER_MINUTE=75` explicitly. Override the env var only if AV changes the
plan ceiling.

**All 25/day free-tier handling has been REMOVED.** The `AV_PRO_TIER` flag is
deleted; there is no daily-quota logic anywhere in the codebase. The only limit
that exists on Pro is the per-minute burst ceiling.

**Rate-limit handling (`_av_fetch`):**
- AV returns HTTP 200 with an `"Information"` or `"Note"` key when the per-minute
  burst ceiling is hit. Hard errors (`"Error Message"`, delisted ticker) are
  distinct and never retried.
- Rate-limit response → **unconditional** exponential backoff (15s / 30s / 60s),
  retrying up to `AV_RATE_LIMIT_RETRIES = 3` times before returning `None`.

**`AV_LAST_FAILURE_REASON`** (module-level in `data_pipeline1.py`) records *why*
the most recent fetch returned nothing, so callers stop guessing:
`rate_limit | api_error | timeout | exception | empty | None`.
Set on every `_av_fetch` failure path and on the empty-`quarterlyReports` path;
read by `run_universe.py` to label failures honestly. Before this existed, any
zero-record fetch was reported as "rate-limit/quota" — which on Pro misattributed
delisted symbols (JNPR acquired by HPE, ZI reticked to GTM, PSTG not found) as
throttling.

**Consecutive-failure stop (`run_universe.py`):** 5 consecutive zero-record
tickers → stop. This is a **structural** failure detector only (dead API key,
bad symbols, network down). Per-minute bursts are absorbed by the `_av_fetch`
retry and never reach the counter.

**Measured Pro throughput:** 16 tickers / 38 AV calls in **115 seconds**, zero
rate-limit responses. The same work took ~2 days on the free tier.

**Other key behaviours:**
- `INSERT OR IGNORE` on all fundamentals rows — idempotent, safe to re-run
- Per-statement skip check: if rows already stored for that ticker+statement, AV
  call is skipped (crucial for partial-load recovery, e.g. INTC IS already stored)
- CFLT removed from universe (taken private 2026 — AV returns 0 records, yfinance
  returns "symbol may be delisted")

### Price data — yfinance
- 5-year daily OHLCV via `yf.download(period="5y")`
- `financialCurrency` field is the currency authority for non-USD exclusion gate
- NOT for commercial use — must migrate in production

### SEC EDGAR (RPO only — Prong 2)
- CIK resolved from `company_tickers.json` (SEC bulk feed, cached locally)
- CRITICAL: accession number prefix ≠ company CIK (prefix is filing agent's CIK
  e.g. 1193125 = Edgar Online). Always look up via `_load_ticker_cik_map()`.
- `sec-edgar-downloader` fetches 10-Qs and 10-Ks into local filing cache
- `fetch_10q_text()` accepts `target_keywords` parameter → keyword-window extraction

### Manual/Automated KPI extraction — metrics_extractor.py (Prong 3)
Extracts: `rpo`, `rpo_prior_year`, NRR, ARR, MAU, ARPU, TCV from 8-K press
releases (primary) and 10-Q fallback.
- Confidence < 0.80 → `needs_review=True`, written to `low_confidence_extractions`
- `rpo_prior_year == rpo` → duplicate sanity check → flag for review
- Output written to `manual_metrics.json`:
  `{ticker: {metric: {value, unit, period, confidence, source_url, needs_review}}}`
- Run with: `python3 metrics_extractor.py --all --force`

## Stage-Change Detection & Alerts

`run_classification()` snapshots the previous `fh_stage` **before** the
`INSERT OR REPLACE`, and on a change:
1. appends to `STAGE_CHANGES_THIS_RUN` (module-level, for run reporting),
2. writes a row to Supabase `stage_change_log`,
3. calls `send_stage_change_alerts()`.

All of it is `try/except` wrapped — an offline run or unconfigured Supabase never
breaks the pipeline. `_get_supabase_client_module()` imports `supabase_client`
with a path fallback into `Valuora_Previous_UI/`.

**Alert emails** — plain text via `smtplib` (Gmail SMTP-SSL, port 465). Queries
Supabase for users with that ticker in their watchlist and
`alert_on_stage_change = TRUE`. Credentials come from `ALERT_EMAIL` /
`ALERT_EMAIL_PASSWORD` (a 16-character Google **App Password**, not the account
password). **Missing or bad credentials → DRY-RUN**: the batch logs
`[DRY-RUN] Would email …` and continues, rather than raising. Verified end to end
with a real send on 2026-07-16.

Emails are sent **by the pipeline running locally**, so the credentials must be
in `~/.zshrc` — putting them only in Streamlit Cloud secrets is not enough.

| | |
|---|--:|
| rows | 11,009 (6,517 active / 4,492 delisted) |

**`in_backtest` rule:** sector is Technology (or a tech-adjacent *industry*)
AND market cap > $1B, OR the ticker is already in `classifications`.

**The 14 delisted names** (human-reviewed, `stage3_review.xlsx`): SPLK, LN,
INFA, OS, EVBG, WKME, ETWO, ENFN, MGIC, MIXT, STEC, SNCR, PET, EPWK.
Five are foreign issuers (LN, MGIC, MIXT, STEC, EPWK) and will likely be

**Survivorship-bias reality check.** All 14 delisted 2024+ except LN (2020).
AV does not retain fundamentals for older delistings: of 362 delisted

**Known caveat — the market-cap floor is biased for delisted names.** Companies

premium acquisitions (winners) and drops failures — the opposite of what
survivorship-bias correction needs. 16 delisted tech names sat below the floor;
6 were admitted by manual review. Consider peak (not final) market cap if this
is revisited.

## AV Data Defects — read before trusting Alpha Vantage output

Hard-won during the 2026-08 universe build. Every one of these silently
produces plausible-looking wrong answers.

**1. AV returns the literal string `"None"`, not JSON null.**
`(ov.get("Sector") or "").strip() or None` lets `"None"` through as if it were
a real value. Use `_av_str()` (in `universe_builder.py`). This silently dropped
~6% of tickers from review lists.

**2. AV freezes delisted tickers — it does not stop serving them.**
After the last real trade the close is carried forward with zero volume,
forever:
```
SPLK 2024-03-15 close=156.90 vol=18,211,874   <- last real day
SPLK 2024-03-18 close=156.90 vol=0            <- Cisco deal closes
SPLK 2026-08-10 close=156.90 vol=0            <- still "trading" 2.4y later
```
One distinct close across 2.4 years. **A backtest reading this holds a flat,
riskless, never-exiting position in a dead company.** This is why
`BACKTEST_RULES.md` Rule 1 exists. Detector: last bar with volume ≥ 1% of the
ticker's median volume (validated within one trading day on SPLK, SGEN, ACIA,
CLDR, ARUN, EVBG).

**3. `LISTING_STATUS` delisting dates are sometimes the file's snapshot date.**
597 of 9,410 rows carried 2026-08-07 instead of the true date (Splunk, Seagen
included). Repaired by `fix_delisting_dates.py`. `universe.delisting_date`
stores the **last tradable day**, deliberately *not* AV's convention (the
following business day), so it can be used as a direct trading cutoff.

**4. AV discards fundamentals for older delisted tickers** (but keeps prices).
Brocade, VASCO, Catalyst Semiconductor, Cloudera, Coupa: zero statements.
Check statement availability *before* spending human review time on a name.

**5. Ticker symbols get reused.** `SOS` = Storage Computer Corp (delisted) →
SOS Ltd (live). Also `BMM`, `PALX`, `RAC`, `SSM`. Splicing a reused symbol
joins two different companies into one price history. 32 such rows are
`in_backtest = 0` with `delisting_date = NULL`.

**6. AV's delisted CSV contains live companies.** `SMTK` and `ASNS` were listed
as delisted while trading millions of shares and filing current financials;
both were corrected to `is_delisted = 0`.

**7. yfinance cannot substitute for delisted data** — it returns ZERO bars for
delisted symbols (verified: SPLK, SGEN, ACIA, CLDR, ARUN). Yahoo purges them.

**8. Substring matching on industry strings is a trap.**
`"BIOTECHNOLOGY"` contains `"TECHNOLOGY"` — this swept 179 biotechs into the
backtest universe (25% of it). But a full `\b…\b` match is ALSO wrong: it
fails on `SEMICONDUCTORS` (AV's plural industry string) and would drop every
chip company. Correct rule = **leading word boundary only**.

**9. AV throws spurious `Invalid API call` errors** for valid symbols under
sustained load; they succeed on retry. `_av_fetch` treats these as hard errors
and does NOT retry — wrap it (see `_av_fetch_retry` in
`backfill_fundamentals.py`).

## Conventions

- All metrics on LTM (last 12 months) basis except balance sheet items (MRQ).
- SBC adjustment applied to FCF BEFORE FCF margin threshold is checked.
- FCF margin adj = (FCF - SBC) / Revenue.
- Cyclical correction: semis use `cycle_adj_pe` (5-yr avg NI), never trailing P/E.
- Standalone modules (metrics_extractor.py) read/write the same SQLite DB.
- `data_pipeline1.py` owns the schema (setup_database). Other files never ALTER.
- Log every LLM call and fallback path for audit trail.
- `as_of_date` = date of most recent fiscal quarter end used in computation.
